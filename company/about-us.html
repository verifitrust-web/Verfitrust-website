FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know about Verifitrust, our process, and what we stand for.

What makes Verifitrust different from other background check providers?

Verifitrust unifies the entire verification workflow — identity, education, employment, criminal, and reference checks — into a single API and dashboard. Instead of juggling dozens of local vendors, you get one contract, one bill, and one consistent candidate experience across 180+ countries.

How long does a typical background check take?

Our average turnaround time is just 1.2 days. Many checks complete within hours thanks to our parallelised screening engine and direct integrations with global databases, courts, and universities. Complex cases may take longer, but you'll always have live status updates.

Is Verifitrust compliant with global data privacy laws?

Yes. We are GDPR, CCPA, and PDPA compliant, and we follow ISO 27001 information security standards. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and candidate information is only retained for the duration required by local regulations.

In which countries can Verifitrust run checks?

We currently operate in 180+ countries through a network of local partners and direct database integrations. Specific check availability varies by jurisdiction — our team will guide you on what's legally permitted in each country.

Do candidates need to install any software?

No. The entire candidate journey runs in a browser or mobile web — no app downloads required. We support document upload, selfie verification, e-signatures, and live status tracking through a single secure link.

Can I integrate Verifitrust with my existing ATS or HRIS?

Absolutely. We offer plug-and-play integrations with Workday, Greenhouse, BambooHR, Lever, SAP SuccessFactors, and more. For custom workflows, our REST API and webhooks let you build any integration in a matter of days.

How is pricing structured?

Pricing is flexible — choose a pay-as-you-go model for low volume, or a packaged subscription for higher volumes with discounted per-check rates. Custom enterprise pricing is available for global teams with specific compliance needs. Talk to our team for a tailored quote.
