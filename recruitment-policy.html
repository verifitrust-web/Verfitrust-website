01 — Introduction

Introduction

This Recruitment Privacy Policy describes the information collection practices that Verifitrust Inc., and its respective subsidiaries ("Verifitrust," "we," "us," or "our") follow when handling Personal Data of prospective candidates applying for employment opportunities.

We are committed to conducting our recruitment process in a transparent, fair, and lawful manner. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your Personal Data when you apply for a position, participate in our recruitment drives, or engage with our Talent Acquisition team.

02 — Scope & Applicability

Scope & Applicability

This Notice outlines how we collect, process, disclose, use, retain, transfer, and protect the Personal Data of candidates who apply to Verifitrust directly, through job portals, recruitment agencies, or via employee referrals.

Important Note

By submitting your resume, application, or Personal Data to Verifitrust, you explicitly consent to the processing of your information for recruitment purposes as outlined in this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this policy, please refrain from submitting your application.

03 — What We Collect

What Personal Data do we collect?

We collect various categories of Personal Data necessary to evaluate your candidacy, communicate with you, and make informed hiring decisions. This includes:

Contact Information

Full name, email address, phone number, current location, and links to professional profiles (e.g., LinkedIn).

Professional Background

Resume/CV details, employment history, educational qualifications, certifications, skills, and work experience.

Assessment Data

Information provided during interviews, technical assessments, written tests, and interactions with our hiring managers.

Background Verification

If an offer is extended, we may collect identity proof, address proof, and references for standard pre-employment background checks.

04 — How We Collect

How we collect your Personal Data

We use different methods to collect candidate Personal Data:

1

Direct Applications

Information you voluntarily submit through our career portal, via email to our HR department, or through job portals where we post openings.

2

Recruitment Agencies

Via third-party recruitment agencies or consultants who share your profile with us for relevant job openings.

3

Public Sources

Publicly available professional platforms like LinkedIn, GitHub, or personal portfolios when assessing passive candidates.

4

HR Events & Forums

Data collected during campus placements, job fairs, networking events, and HR forums.

05 — Purpose & Legal Basis

Purpose & Legal Basis

The purposes for which we process your Personal Data, along with the legal basis for each:

Purpose Legal Basis
Evaluating qualifications, skills, and suitability for applied or potential roles
Necessary for taking steps at your request prior to entering into an employment contract.
Communicating with you regarding your application, interviews, and results
Justified on the basis of our legitimate interest in managing the recruitment process.
Conducting pre-employment background verification checks
Based on the explicit consent provided by the candidate and necessary for compliance with legal obligations.
Maintaining records of the hiring process for audit and compliance
Necessary for compliance with applicable labor laws and regulations.
Considering you for future job opportunities that match your profile

06 — Data Sharing

To whom we share your Personal Data

Your Personal Data is treated with strict confidentiality. We do not sell your data. Access is restricted to authorized personnel only. However, we may share your information under the following circumstances:

Internal Access

Shared strictly on a need-to-know basis with HR personnel, hiring managers, and interview panelists involved in the specific recruitment drive.

External Service Providers

  • Background Check Agencies: If an offer is extended, we may share your details with our trusted verification partners to conduct background checks (subject to your explicit consent).
  • Technology Providers: We use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) and CRM tools (hosted on secure platforms like AWS) to manage candidate data efficiently.
  • Statutory Bodies: If required by law, court order, or governmental authority.
07 — Retention Period

Retention Period

We will retain your Personal Data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.

If you are hired by Verifitrust, your candidate data will be transferred to your employee file and retained according to our Employee Records Policy. If you are not selected, we will retain your application data for a maximum of 24 months to consider you for future opportunities, unless you request earlier deletion.

08 — Candidate Rights

Your Rights as a Candidate

As a candidate, you have the following rights regarding the Personal Data we process, subject to applicable data protection laws:

Right of Access

Request a copy of the Personal Data we hold about you.

Right to Rectification

Correct any inaccurate or incomplete information in your application.

Right to Erasure

Request the deletion of your Personal Data from our talent pool.

Right to Object

Object to the processing of your data for future recruitment purposes.

Right to Portability

Obtain and reuse your data across different services.

Right to Withdraw

Withdraw consent for background checks or talent pool retention at any time.
