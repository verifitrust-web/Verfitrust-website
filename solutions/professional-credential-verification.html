The journey to verified credentials

From a confident "I'm fully qualified" to a verified professional walking into the role — here's how Verifitrust screens, validates, and confirms every credential with global authorities.

Step 01

"I'm certified and fully qualified for this role."

Every great hire starts with a confident claim. The candidate brings the resume, certifications, licenses, and memberships to the table — and the hiring team is ready to take the next step: verifying that every credential is real, current, and earned.

Step 02

Secure submission. No back-and-forth.

The candidate uploads certifications, licenses, memberships, and specialized skills through a secure, encrypted submission portal. Consent is captured upfront — and the moment documents land, the verification engine kicks in.

Step 03

A global network of issuing authorities.

Universities, licensing boards, certification bodies, and professional associations — Verifitrust taps into a connected global verification network to confirm each credential directly at its source. No fake diplomas. No expired licenses. No silent mismatches.

Valid credentials confirmed. Mismatches flagged.

As responses arrive, the dashboard lights up: education verified, certifications confirmed, licenses valid. Anything expired, mismatched, or unverifiable is flagged as a Verification Alert — so your team reviews the full picture before making the call.

Verified qualifications. Trusted expertise. The candidate's profile now carries the Verifitrust seal — backed by direct confirmation from issuing authorities. Hiring teams move forward with clarity, and candidates feel their hard work is recognized.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about professional credential verification, global authority checks, and compliance.

Professional credential verification is the process of validating a candidate's certifications, licenses, educational degrees, professional memberships, and specialized skills directly with the issuing authorities. It confirms that the credentials presented by a candidate are authentic, current, and issued by recognized institutions — helping employers hire with confidence and reduce regulatory risk across every role and region.

We verify a wide range of professional credentials including university degrees and academic transcripts, professional certifications (e.g., CPA, PMP, AWS, Cisco), occupational licenses (medical, legal, engineering), professional body memberships, and specialized technical skills. Each verification is sourced directly from the issuing institution or an authorized global verification network — never self-reported or third-party aggregated.

Most credential verifications are completed within 24 to 72 hours. Verifications that require direct response from international universities, licensing boards, or professional bodies may take up to 5–7 business days depending on the institution's response time. Real-time status updates are visible on your dashboard throughout the entire process, with no manual follow-up required.

Yes. Our verification process is fully compliant with FCRA, EEOC, GDPR, and regional hiring regulations worldwide. Candidate consent is captured before any verification begins, and we only collect and process data that is job-relevant, proportionate, and necessary for the hiring decision. Every step is logged for audit and regulatory review.

If a credential cannot be verified, our system surfaces it as a Verification Alert with the reason — such as expired license, mismatched issuing authority, unverifiable institution, or fraudulent document. Flagged items are reviewed by trained analysts before reaching your hiring team, and candidates are given the opportunity to provide additional documentation or clarification through a structured dispute workflow.

Absolutely. Under FCRA and similar global regulations, candidates have the right to review flagged results and dispute inaccuracies before any adverse hiring decision is finalized. Verifitrust provides a structured pre-adverse action workflow, allowing candidates to submit supporting documents, correct misidentifications, or clarify expired credentials — ensuring a fair, transparent, and auditable process for both employer and candidate.
