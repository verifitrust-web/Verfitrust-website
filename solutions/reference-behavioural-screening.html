The Journey

The journey to behavioural trust

From a strong resume to a confident, people-first hiring decision. Follow how reference & behavioural screening adds the right layer of insight — fairly, transparently, and only when it matters.

Step 01

Strong profile. Right skills. But what about the team?

The candidate walks in with the experience and the skills. But great people build great teams — so before the offer, there's one more critical layer: understanding how they actually work with others, communicate under pressure, and take ownership on the job.

Step 02

Who can speak to your professional journey?

The candidate provides professional references — typically former managers or direct supervisors who can speak to their role, responsibilities, and working style. Consent is captured upfront, and every reference is contacted only after the candidate gives explicit permission.

Step 03

Structured reference calls. The right questions.

Our team connects with each reference through a structured call — covering role and responsibilities, working relationship, communication style, and teamwork. Every question is job-relevant, consistent across candidates, and designed to surface real behavioural insight — not opinions or gossip.

Step 04

Professional behaviour, assessed with context.

References share how the candidate communicated, handled challenges, collaborated, and took ownership. We capture the full context — not just ratings — so your team gets a nuanced, human picture of how the person actually works, not a checkbox scorecard.

Step 05

Verified experience. Contextual insight.

The screening report surfaces whether the candidate's claimed experience aligns with what references describe — including responsibilities, working style, and any areas that might need additional clarification. Every finding is tied to a specific reference, with full context for your team to review.

Step 06

Verified experience.
Confident decision.

With reference feedback and behavioural insight in hand, the candidate is welcomed aboard. Your team moves forward with trust — knowing the person joining brings not just the right skills, but the right working style, communication, and cultural alignment for the role.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about reference & behavioural screening and how it helps you hire people who fit — not just on paper, but in practice.

Reference & behavioural screening is the process of contacting a candidate's provided professional references — typically former managers, colleagues, or supervisors — to verify their role, responsibilities, performance, communication style, teamwork, and overall professional conduct. It is conducted with candidate consent and focuses strictly on job-relevant behavioural insights, never personal lifestyle or protected attributes.

Reference & behavioural screening is recommended for nearly all roles, especially those requiring collaboration, leadership, client interaction, or cultural alignment. It is particularly valuable for mid-to-senior level positions, team-critical roles, and positions where interpersonal dynamics directly impact team performance, client relationships, and workplace culture.

Most reference & behavioural screenings are completed within 48 to 96 hours, depending on the number of references provided, their availability, and time zones.
