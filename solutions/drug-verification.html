Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about drug screening, substance testing, and compliance for your hiring process.

Drug and substance screening is the process of testing biological samples—such as urine, hair, saliva, or blood—to detect the presence of controlled substances or prescription medications. It helps employers maintain a safe, compliant, and responsible workplace for everyone on the team.

Standard 5-panel tests include marijuana (THC), cocaine, opiates, amphetamines, and PCP. Expanded panels (7, 10, or 12-panel) can also detect benzodiazepines, barbiturates, synthetic opioids, and alcohol, depending on your industry and regulatory requirements.

Sample collection typically takes 15–30 minutes at the authorized center. Negative results are usually available within 24–48 hours. Non-negative results that require a confirmed laboratory review by a Medical Review Officer (MRO) may take 3–5 business days.
