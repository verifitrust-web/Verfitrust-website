Step 03

Searching across court records, public databases, and government sources.

The system moves from Initiated → Searching → Verifying → Compiling Results → Completed. Every check is run against trusted judicial and government sources for complete coverage.

Step 04

Wait… a potential match?

A potential match is detected in one of the data sources. The system flags it for review—but a flag is not a conclusion. It simply means the details require closer human analysis.

Our analysts verify identity, case status, date, and relevance. Many flagged matches turn out to be name collisions or dismissed cases—so every record is reviewed before a final conclusion is reached.

Criminal and court verification is the process of checking a candidate's history against court records, public record databases, and government sources to identify any criminal cases, convictions, or ongoing legal proceedings. It helps employers make informed, risk-aware hiring decisions.

We check district and high court records, public record databases, and verified government sources. Our system searches across civil and criminal case databases to surface any adverse records tied to the candidate's identity.

Digital database matches return results instantly. Manual court-record verifications that require outreach to court clerks typically take 3 to 7 business days, depending on the jurisdiction and the court's response time.
