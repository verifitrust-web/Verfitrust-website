Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about social media verification and responsible digital screening.

Social media verification is the process of reviewing a candidate's publicly available social media activity to flag content that may pose reputational, compliance, or workplace culture risks. It complements traditional background checks and helps employers make fair, informed, and consistent hiring decisions — without intruding on private or protected content.

We review publicly available content on major platforms such as LinkedIn, X (Twitter), Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube. Private, password-protected, or access-restricted content is never reviewed, and we strictly follow FCRA, EEOC, and regional data privacy regulations throughout the screening process.

Most social media verifications are completed within 24 to 72 hours, depending on the number of platforms reviewed and the volume of public content associated with the candidate. Real-time status updates are visible on your dashboard throughout the process, with no manual follow-up required.

Yes. Our screening is fully compliant with FCRA, EEOC, GDPR, and local hiring regulations. We only assess job-relevant content using consistent, unbiased criteria, and candidates are informed and consent to the screening before any review begins. Protected classes, religious views, political opinions, and other sensitive attributes are never used in hiring decisions.

When a potential risk indicator is detected, our system surfaces the flagged post, timestamp, and surrounding context for human review. A trained analyst evaluates whether the signal is genuinely job-relevant before it ever reaches your hiring team. We never auto-reject candidates — final decisions always remain with your team, supported by transparent context.

Absolutely. Under FCRA and similar regulations, candidates have the right to review flagged content and dispute inaccuracies before any hiring decision is finalized.
