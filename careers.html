FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about working at Verifitrust.

Yes, Verifitrust is a remote-first company. While we have physical offices in Chennai, India, the majority of our team works remotely across the globe. We believe in hiring the best talent regardless of location, as long as there is overlap with your team's working hours.

Our interview process typically takes 2-3 weeks. It starts with a 30-minute phone screen with HR to discuss your background and the role. This is followed by a technical or role-specific assessment, a deep-dive interview with the hiring manager, and a final culture-fit interview with the leadership team.

We evaluate visa sponsorships on a case-by-case basis depending on the role, the candidate's location, and business needs. We are committed to securing top global talent and will discuss relocation or sponsorship options early in the screening process if applicable.

We offer comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance, unlimited PTO, a home office stipend, continuous learning budgets, and stock options for all full-time employees. Specifics may vary depending on your geographic location.
