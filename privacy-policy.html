03 — Definitions

Definitions

Understanding the key terms used throughout this Privacy Policy.

Applicable Data Protection Law

Relevant local and/or international laws/regulations based on the jurisdiction where the individual resides or is located. Notable laws include the GDPR and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA).

Personal Data

Any data relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

Sensitive Personal Data

A specific set of "special categories" that must be treated with extra security, as may be required under Applicable Data Protection Law.

Data Subject / Data Principal

Any identified or identifiable natural person — one who can be identified directly or indirectly by reference to an identification number or specific factors.

Processing

Any operation performed on Personal Data — whether automated or not — including collection, recording, storage, alteration, retrieval, use, transfer, disclosure, alignment, restriction, erasure, or destruction.

Data Controller / Data Fiduciary

A person, company, or other body which determines the purposes and means of processing the Personal Data.

Consent

Any freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous indication of an individual's permission by which they signify agreement to the processing of their Personal Data.

Data Processor & Sub-processor

A person, company, or other body who processes data on behalf of a Data Controller. A Sub-processor assists the Data Processor in its processing activities.

Personal Data Breach

A security event leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to Personal Data.
